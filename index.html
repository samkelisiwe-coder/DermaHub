Our mission

DermaHub was built to make professional somatology knowledge accessible — whether you are a student learning the fundamentals, a practising therapist looking for a quick clinical reference, or a client trying to understand your skin better.

We believe that informed clients make better decisions about their skin health, and that somatologists deserve a modern, reliable reference tool they can trust.

Important disclaimer

DermaHub is an educational and informational reference tool. It does not replace professional consultation with a qualified and registered somatologist. Always seek advice from a licensed professional before starting any treatment.

Built for South Africa

DermaHub is designed with the South African somatology context in mind — including treatment recommendations relevant to melanin-rich skin, local industry standards and the demographics of South African clients.
